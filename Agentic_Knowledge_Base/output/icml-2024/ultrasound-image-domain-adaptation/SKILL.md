# Ultrasound Image Domain Adaptation

Unsupervised domain adaptation techniques (e.g., adversarial training, image translation) to transfer models trained on one ultrasound dataset/scanner to another without target-domain labels, specifically for detecting and localizing anatomical structures. Addresses the challenge of distribution shift across different ultrasound acquisition settings, probes, or patient populations.
