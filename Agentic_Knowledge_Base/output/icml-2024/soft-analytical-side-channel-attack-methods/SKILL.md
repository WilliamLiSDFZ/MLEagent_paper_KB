# Soft Analytical Side Channel Attack Methods

Soft analytical side-channel attacks (SASCA) that leverage tractable circuit representations (e.g., arithmetic circuits or probabilistic graphical models compiled into tractable forms) to perform **exact** probabilistic inference over leakage distributions, enabling precise computation of posterior key probabilities without approximation errors that plague belief propagation-based methods.
