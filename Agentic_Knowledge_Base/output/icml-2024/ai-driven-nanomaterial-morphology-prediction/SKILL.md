# Ai Driven Nanomaterial Morphology Prediction

**AI-driven nanomaterial morphology prediction** covers the application of machine learning and deep learning models to predict the shape, structure, and geometric features (e.g., nanoparticles, nanorods, nanosheets) of nanomaterials based on synthesis conditions, chemical composition, or precursor parameters. This skill encompasses training predictive models on experimental or simulation datasets
