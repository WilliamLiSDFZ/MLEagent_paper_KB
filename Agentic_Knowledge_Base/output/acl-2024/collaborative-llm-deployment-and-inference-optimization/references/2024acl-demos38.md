# LlamaFactory: Unified Efficient Fine-Tuning of 100+ Language Models

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-demos.38/](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-demos.38/)

**TLDR**: LlamaFactory is a unified framework supporting efficient fine-tuning of over 100 language models with state-of-the-art training methods.

## Abstract

AbstractEfficient fine-tuning is vital for adapting large language models (LLMs) to downstream tasks. However, it requires non-trivial efforts to implement these methods on different models. We present LlamaFactory, a unified framework that integrates a suite of cutting-edge efficient training methods. It provides a solution for flexibly customizing the fine-tuning of 100+ LLMs without the need for coding through the built-in web UI LlamaBoard. We empirically validate the efficiency and effectiveness of our framework on language modeling and text generation tasks. It has been released at https://github.com/hiyouga/LLaMA-Factory and received over 25,000 stars and 3,000 forks.
