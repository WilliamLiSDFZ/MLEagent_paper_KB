# CMDL: A Large-Scale Chinese Multi-Defendant Legal Judgment Prediction Dataset

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.351/](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.351/)

**TLDR**: Introduces CMDL, a large-scale Chinese dataset for legal judgment prediction in complex multi-defendant cases.

## Abstract

AbstractLegal Judgment Prediction (LJP) has attracted significant attention in recent years. However, previous studies have primarily focused on cases involving only a single defendant, skipping multi-defendant cases due to complexity and difficulty. To advance research, we introduce CMDL, a large-scale real-world Chinese Multi-Defendant LJP dataset, which consists of over 393,945 cases with nearly 1.2 million defendants in total. For performance evaluation, we propose case-level evaluation metrics dedicated for the multi-defendant scenario. Experimental results on CMDL show existing SOTA approaches demonstrate weakness when applied to cases involving multiple defendants. We highlight several challenges that require attention and resolution.
