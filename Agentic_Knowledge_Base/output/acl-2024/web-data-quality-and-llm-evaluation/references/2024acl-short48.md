# Shoulders of Giants: A Look at the Degree and Utility of Openness in NLP Research

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-short.48/](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-short.48/)

**TLDR**: An analysis of ACL Anthology papers quantifies openness practices and the benefits of open culture in NLP research.

## Abstract

AbstractWe analysed a sample of NLP research papers archived in ACL Anthology as an attempt to quantify the degree of openness and the benefit of such an open culture in the NLP community. We observe that papers published in different NLP venues show different patterns related to artefact reuse. We also note that more than 30% of the papers we analysed do not release their artefacts publicly. Further, we observe a wide language-wise disparity in publicly available NLP-related artefacts.
