# On the Hallucination in Simultaneous Machine Translation

**Source**: [https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-short.66/](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-short.66/)

**TLDR**: This paper analyzes the causes and characteristics of hallucinations in simultaneous machine translation caused by incomplete source information.

## Abstract

AbstractIt is widely known that hallucination is a critical issue in Simultaneous Machine Translation (SiMT) due to the absence of source-side information. While many efforts have been made to enhance performance for SiMT, few of them attempt to understand and analyze hallucination in SiMT.Therefore, we conduct a comprehensive analysis of hallucination in SiMT from two perspectives: understanding the distribution of hallucination words and the target-side context usage of them.Intensive experiments demonstrate some valuable findings and particularly show that it is possible to alleviate hallucination by decreasing the over usage of target-side information for SiMT.
